# OSCAR Product

## Product Statement

OSCAR Terminal helps traders turn raw market data into consistent, explainable decisions.

## Product Goals

- Improve decision consistency.
- Centralize institutional-style market interpretation.
- Provide transparent rationale for each signal.
- Support iterative strategy development.

## Scope

- Decision center dashboard
- Smart money visual support
- Institutional analysis pipeline
- Validation and backtest workflows
- Launcher-driven local operation

## Non-Goals

- Fully autonomous trade execution.
- Undocumented black-box decisioning.

## Version Direction

- Alpha phases: stabilization of architecture and workflows.
- Beta phases: usability hardening and analytics depth.
- 1.0: production-grade operating model and documentation maturity.
